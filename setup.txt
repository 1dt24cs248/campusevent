INSTITUTIONAL IDENTITIES
    Management can provision student and faculty accounts from the Student Management
    and Faculty Management pages. Login accepts college email, student USN/roll number,
    or faculty employee ID, plus the provisioned password. Existing demo accounts
    continue to use their demo.local email addresses.
    Student Dashboard, My Profile, My Academics, and My Timetable use the signed-in
    student's own identity and section schedule. Subjects come from that section's
    timetable assignments; syllabus, resources, attendance, and marks remain empty
    unless they are actually published. Synthetic timetable/subject data is labelled.

FACULTY TEACHING PORTAL
    Faculty sign in with their provisioned employee ID or college email. Their
    profile, assigned subjects/sections, teaching timetable, room changes, and
    notifications are scoped to the authenticated faculty identity.
    Faculty may start attendance for an assigned class, mark the section roster,
    and review their session history. Student attendance shows only explicitly
    recorded present/absent entries; missing attendance is not treated as absence.
    Classroom requests are restricted to assigned subject/section pairs. Management
    must confirm a room, and approval validates capacity, room status/type/facilities,
    room unavailability, and room/faculty/section timetable conflicts. Approved
    extra classes are surfaced through the existing timetable APIs for the affected
    faculty and section, and notifications are sent to both audiences.

ACADEMIC ERP
    Student academics are scoped to the signed-in student's section. Published
    syllabus modules, section-specific resources, assessments, marks, attendance
    summaries, and role/section-targeted announcements are shown only when records
    exist. Draft assessments and their marks are hidden from students until the
    faculty member publishes the assessment. Faculty may add syllabus modules,
    resources, assessments, and marks only for timetable-authorized subjects and
    sections. Management Academic Management is a read-only master-data directory
    plus a publisher for academic announcements; student-section assignments remain
    managed through Student Management. New faculty-entered records inherit synthetic
    provenance from synthetic academic assignments and are labelled in the portal.
    No academic samples are seeded into the campus database.

EVENT MANAGEMENT
    Faculty can propose events for students, faculty, or both. Proposals remain
    private to their creator and management until management approves and publishes
    them. Students and faculty see only published upcoming events for their audience.
    Event decisions and publication notifications use the existing scoped notification
    system. Startup adds nullable event metadata columns to legacy databases without
    rewriting existing event rows.

CAMPUS FOOD / CANTEEN
    Management creates food stalls linked to existing active campus rooms, then
    creates dated menus with item descriptions, categories, prices, dietary notes,
    and availability. New menus stay private as drafts until explicitly published;
    unpublishing hides them from student and faculty APIs. Students and faculty
    can browse only published menus by campus-local date. No sample stalls or
    food menus are seeded. Existing food-item rows are retained and migrated into
    published legacy menus so previously visible entries are not lost. Startup
    adds food metadata columns and the new food_menus table additively.

DATABASE SAFETY
    The reset_demo scripts delete the selected campus.db and recreate synthetic data.
    Use them only with a disposable demo database after confirming its configured path.
    Never use them with a shared, production, or real-campus database. Schema upgrades
    performed by app startup are additive and preserve existing rows. Faculty phase
    adds new classroom-request and attendance-session/record tables; event management
    adds nullable metadata columns to the existing events table. Academic startup
    adds nullable subject description/credit metadata, assessment metadata and
    publication status, and an optional announcement section scope. It creates
    syllabus_modules, academic_resources, and academic_marks tables; attendance
    reuses the existing attendance tables. Legacy assessments are retained and
    treated as published for compatibility. Neither phase resets existing timetable,
    student, notification, scenario, or published-version data.

RUN THE CHECKS
    Never run validation with DATABASE_URL pointing to backend/campus.db. For
    isolated SQLite pytest runs in PowerShell:
      cd backend
      $env:DATABASE_URL = "sqlite://"

      pytest test_student_portal.py test_faculty_portal.py test_identity.py test_events.py test_food.py test_actions.py

    a dedicated backend port, and delete only that validation database afterward.

STEP 12 - TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "ortools" fails to install: use Python 3.11 or 3.12 and upgrade pip first (python -m pip install --upgrade pip).
- PowerShell blocks venv activation: Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass, then activate again.
- Frontend cannot reach the API / CORS error: backend must run on port 8000; frontend on 5173; Vite proxy maps /api.
- Port already in use: close the old process or change the port in both the start command and vite.config.ts.
- Login fails after reseeding: clear the browser's local storage (the old token is invalid) and log in again.
- Solver slow: raise or lower SOLVER_TIME_LIMIT_SEC in backend/.env; the problem is small and should finish in seconds.
- Dates look wrong ("tomorrow" has no classes): tomorrow may be Sunday; the UI uses the next working day. You can also set the demo clock in Admin.
- Antigravity stops mid-build: use the "Continue from the first unchecked milestone" message.
